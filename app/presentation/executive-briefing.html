Design principle

Surround, not replace.

GroundStar, G-Ops, Mosaic, DSM, iFleet, BRS and the CAG feeds stay the systems of record. The control tower reads from them first, and writes back later.

  • Protects existing investment and the people who run it.
  • One trusted source of operational truth for every view, prediction and report.
  • Integrate once, instead of rebuilding for every new initiative.

Act 4 · Prototyping

Not a slide. Working software.

  • Role lenses: Control Tower Lead to Ramp Lead, same facts, different focus.
  • Linked 2D and 3D: select one delayed flight, and its stand, tasks, GSE and exceptions light up everywhere.
  • What-if: a 45-minute late inbound and a weather stoppage, compared with the untouched baseline.
  • One-click optimiser: re-plans stands, GSE and crew within SLA priorities and limits, and shows its reasons.
  • Day and week planning: gates, teams, GSE and belts, with conflicts flagged.
